So you want to understand History?
Overview▼❦HumanitiesHistoryPhilosophyReligious studiesArts & music▶⚖Social sciences▶☉Natural sciences▶∑Formal sciences▶⚒Applied sciences
Did Rome fall, or just change?Real collapse in the West, from politics, invasion and climate; the East carried on.
Great men or deep structures?Leaders matter most where constraints are weak; structures set the menu.
Why did the West get rich first?No single factor wins: wages, coal, empire and ideas all mattered.
Did religion fight science?Historians dropped the war story; real clashes were local.
Why did Latin America fall behind?Institutions lead; dependency theory kept a point but lost the whole.
Was there a Scientific Revolution?The label survives; the one-event, one-genius version is dead.
Is the world heading for too many people?Too few is now the bigger worry in most of the world.

So you’re arguing about Socrates?
Overview▼ΩAncientsSocratesPlatoAristotleStoicismEpicurus▶∃Metaphysics▶ΨMind▶§Ethics▶†Religion▶⚑Politics
“The unexamined life is not worth living”Plenty of people live decent lives without scrutiny.Examining things is what got him executed.Socrates lost his own trial.
“He knew that he knew nothing”The modesty is selective.If he knew nothing, he didn’t know that either.“I know nothing,” then corrected people for fifty years.
“He died for his principles”The sources are admirers writing afterwards.He was offered exile and turned it down.He picked hemlock to make a point.
“No one does wrong knowingly”Weakness of will is one of the most familiar things about people.Everyone who has eaten a second dessert knew.He’d never met Athenians.
“The Socratic method gets at the truth”The early dialogues mostly end in a shrug.A method that worked would finish at some point.It’s asking “but what is justice?” until they leave.

So you’re sure about abortion?
Overview▼⚕HealthAbortion and end of lifeBodies and consentMental healthVaccines and COVID▶⚖Crime and justice▶¤Economics▶✎Education▶☘Environment
You’ve heard “Late-term abortions are common”CDC data for 2022: 40% happened by six weeks, 7% after the first trimester.
You’ve heard “Most Americans support abortion rights”Gallup 2023: 69% back legality in the first three months, 22% in the last.

You’ve heard “Abortion patients are mostly young and childless”About six in ten had already given birth at least once.
You’ve heard “Death is a medical fact everyone agrees on”US law has defined it since 1981, but a 2023 revision effort stalled.
You’ve heard “Organs are only taken once the donor is dead”A 2025 federal review flagged 351 cases for a closer look.
You’ve heard “Overturning Roe cut the number of abortions”Monthly totals rose in 2023 and 2024, helped by telehealth.
