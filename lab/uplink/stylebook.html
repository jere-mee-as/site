The realism half. One object per screen is lit in real 3D, rendered at a fifth of its size, then reduced to two or three inks through a 4×4 ordered dither and scaled up with hard pixels. The light is real and the ink is flat. Everything else on the screen stays flat graphics.

09/12Motion + sound · one slot change, frame by frame at 30fpsMeasured from the UI reel動き・音
Rules

The time base is 33ms. Cuts are hard. Nothing eases, fades or lifts on hover. Panels arrive by block resolve in six steps, and list rows arrive one per tick. A slot change runs about 270ms of graphic cards, then cuts. At most three big brightness jumps per burst, and reduced motion gets one clean cut.

Sound follows actions only. Hovering is silent. Audio wakes on the first key or click, and [M] mutes.

The hangar window is a panel on the shell screen that loops these spots with hard cuts, held 2.5 to 3 seconds each, the way the faction intros hold. One ad card also flashes inside every slot-change burst. That is all the street gets. The hijack concept, where you fight through ads to reach the content, is parked for its own pass.

The corporations, marks and slogans are all invented. No Marathon, Bungie or faction marks appear anywhere. Chinese belongs to the agri-corp's packaging, and Japanese to the genetics firm and the shell's own co-labels.

SHELL SCHEMATIC · 04SLICE-LOFTED · RIG 11 · DRAG TO TURN

The wireframe is the finished picture, not a debug view.

Runners are synthetic shells, so the hangar shows yours as a scan. It takes the shell screen's one object slot. It turns slowly, you can drag it, and it resolves top-down on entry. A single scan band sweeps it every 7.5 seconds in the shell's signal color, which means Paint recolors the scan.

MESHfront faces, white at 72%. Lat-long grid split into triangles, alternating diagonals. KEYorange key light from the left, in camera space. Each triangle gets its own threshold, so the edge of the light breaks up like dither. CRADLEorange lattice behind the skull, the cortex cradle. Squares, never triangles. RIGrig blue, used only here: bones drawn x-ray, joints as rings with three axis ticks, optics filled solid. SCANone band in the signal color. Never more than one at a time. BACKback faces at 8.5%. Enough to read volume without turning to noise.

The head is lofted from horizontal slices that follow real proportions. Eyes sit at half the head's height, and the face divides into thirds: hairline to brow, brow to nose base, nose base to chin. The ears span brow to nose base and sit behind the jaw hinge. The neck leans into the trapezius. It is drawn from scratch and resembles no particular person or character. Callouts use Kode Mono on carbon slugs and sort themselves so they never overlap.
