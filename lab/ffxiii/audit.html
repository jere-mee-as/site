How close is the preview to FFXIII?

The title sequence, the cursor and the cloud drift already feel right. The menus don't yet: the rows are the wrong material, the footer is the wrong colour, the Datalog is a web page rather than a log, and the big screen changes white out the whole frame where the game keeps its chrome and moves only the content. Everything below is measured from the five new clips and 28 stills, compared against a recording of the preview, and each fix has a working mockup you can play.

Field
Not dark. Mid grey-violet marble covered in fine white scratches, with a white-hot bloom about a third of the screen wide behind the current stage and a purple-grey vignette at the edges. It reads like smoked stone lit from inside. A darker strip sits just under the top rule.
Stages
Each stage is a closed loop of thick glassy teal ribbon, bright on its outer edge and darker on the inner one. Acquired arcs are bright; the rest of the loop is a faint ghost. Thin dark spokes with a gold glint run from the nodes to the stage crystal in the middle, a rough violet-white gem standing on an engraved dish. Under it, a glowing outer band with inward spikes and small key glyphs, translucent hex plates outside it. All stages hang on one dark thread that crosses the whole screen.
Nodes
Acquired nodes are small glowing spheres in their colour; unacquired ones are dark metal. Slate chips (HP: +10) label the selected node and its two neighbours. The hand points at the node from the left, and small white chevrons on the ribbon show which ways you can move.
Overview
Toggle zoom pulls back under a white flash in ~250 ms. The camera looks down the thread, every node becomes a small coloured ring icon, and the stages read as a tunnel of rings around the bloom, drifting slowly. The left panel lists the six roles with Lv., the hand on one, and a Medic Crystals table under a rule (clip 29 @ 2.6s, 23.2s).
Moves
Choosing a role collapses the list to that one row at once and the camera swoops into its stage in ~900 ms (4.65–5.6s). Moving node to node is a ~600 ms camera glide along the ribbon (11.2–11.8s). Acquiring a node is a white bloom on the node, violet hexagon bokeh rising off the disc, and the slate notice Maximum HP increased by 10!, in ~100 ms, held ~0.9 s, out in ~100 ms (11.8–12.7s).
Preview
Light grey-lavender field, one thin white helix line through every report, thin elliptical rings, glossy orbs with a tall beam on the selected one, teal chips, the full main menu on the left and a silver-bevelled card with a blue Open capsule.
Mapping
Roles become report types (Connectome Lv. 4, Artifact Lv. 1 …). Stages become months: one loop per month, six slots, future slots dark. Nodes are reports, and the stage crystal is the month. On the card, HP/CP become No./Stage, Strength/Magic become Logged/Type, ATB LEVEL becomes Archive level. CP COST becomes Index. Advance crystogenesis becomes Enter: Read report, and the first read of a report is the acquisition, with Crystal acquired: …!
Change
Replace the helix with stacked month loops on a thread. Replace the light field with the marble, the bloom and the vignette. Use the overview as the Crystarium's landing view with the type list, so it opens the way the game does, and dive into a stage on Enter. Returning visitors can skip straight to the newest stage. Drop the beam, the blue capsule and the main menu on this screen.

5. Typography and small parts

Game
One squared, wide sans for everything, white with a 1 px dark outline. Micro labels are wide caps. Numbers are wide italics with an outline. The title logo is the only place with a serif-like face (refs 13, 24).
Preview
Instrument Serif appears inside the Datalog and Crystarium card titles. Text on bars uses a soft drop shadow, so it goes muddy on the silver.
Change
Serif only on the title wordmark. Menu text: Saira width 112, weight 500, with text-shadow: 0 0 1px #000, 0 1px 1px #000. Numbers: Saira width 125 italic with the same outline; keep the gold only for the big count-up. Keycaps dark (black pill, white letter), like the L1/R1 glyphs in ref 28.
Title
Keep the sequence as built. The sigil's thick ribbons read as marker strokes next to the reference's fine ink lines and wash. Halve the ribbon widths, add two or three hairline strokes, and lower the fill opacity of the shard to about 0.7 so the halo shows through.
