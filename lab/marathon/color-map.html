JRL Color Map

Jere.ing color map

Every module in both modes, with the locked type (Hubot Sans: wordmark 500 / 75%, titles 300 / 88%, slogans 200). The numbers under each screen are contrast ratios for the text actually placed on each color. Small text needs 4.5 or more; type at 24px and up needs 3.

  • 01The system layer (header, terminal, feed, boot, sound chip) never takes a module color. Dark mode: black, white, lime. White mode: fog and black, with lime only as a solid strip.
  • 02Each module gets one field, one ink and at most one accent. No tints of the field. Two hues on screen at most, plus the system layer.
  • 03Small text only ever sits on black, white or fog. Red, pink, orange and lavender are mid-tones that fail small text in both black and white, so they carry big shapes and large type only.
  • 04Red means something is wrong: errors, kernel panic. Yellow with black means warning (hazard stripes). Neither is a module color.
  • 05Lime and yellow vanish on white (contrast about 1:1), so in white mode they only appear as fills with black text.
  • 06More hues than that appear only in transitions and the idle palette cycle, which are brief and built from the two modules involved.

Dark · system register

Black chrome, saturated fields. Modeled on the UI reel and the Alpha and Story intros.

White · faction register

Fog field, black ink, one accent per module, as each faction intro has one color.

Changes from the live site: pink #ff0088 becomes #e0216f; Babel text moves onto black slabs; Culture goes to teal dots on black (dark) and blue on fog (white); red is reserved for errors.
