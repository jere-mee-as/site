System A is locked: Kode Mono for codes and numbers, JetBrains Mono for interface text, Doto for the clock, EB Garamond for rare accent lines. This page swaps only the display face, set lighter and narrower than before. Wide, black Archivo is kept for one job only: giant words cropped off the edge. The sliders change every row at once. Weight is capped per family (Instrument Sans tops out at 700).
