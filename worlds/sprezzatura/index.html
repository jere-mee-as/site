“…which conceals all art.”Baldassare Castiglione · 1528

ii.Che cos’è

Mastery, made to look like it cost nothing.

Not only for men, and not only for clothes: a way of carrying anything.

An Italian word for studied nonchalance: doing something well while hiding that it took work, so it looks natural.

Today it is used mostly for menswear. The idea itself has nothing to do with gender. It is about how a thing is worn, said or done, not about what the thing is.

The Book of the Courtier is set over four evenings at the court of Urbino in 1507. Castiglione worked on it for about twenty years, and the Aldine Press in Venice printed it in 1528.

The ideal courtier should do difficult things as if they cost him no thought. Castiglione needed a new word for that, so he coined one.

Thomas Hoby’s English translation of 1561 turned it into “recklessness”.

vi.Sulle spalle

Thrown over the shoulders, sleeves empty. Same gesture, either wardrobe.

A jacket worn over the shoulders, or a sweater knotted loosely at the chest. It is the most copied move in Italian street style, and it works the same on a cashmere crew neck as on a tailored jacket.

Florence during the menswear fairs is where to watch it done well, and done badly.

viii.Cosa la uccide

  1. The pre-tied bow tie.
  2. The pre-folded pocket square.
  3. The tie and square, sold as a set.

Instead: squares pushed in by hand, ties that don’t match, nothing bought as a set.

The Aesthetics Wiki’s list of what kills it: the pre-tied bow tie, the pre-folded square, the matching tie-and-square set. All three are ease that someone else did for you.

A planned flaw doesn’t fix it either. Forced imperfection looks just as fake. What works is doing it by hand and leaving it a little loose.

ix.La strada

Its stage is the street, and the trap is the lens.

Pitti Uomo is the menswear fair held in Florence every January and June, mostly in the Fortezza da Basso. Street photographers have made its courtyards and piazzas the look’s main stage since the 2000s.

That is also the trap. Once you are dressing for the lens, the ease is gone. The ones worth circling look as if they haven’t noticed the camera.

x.Il colore

Colour, taken lightly. A clash is only a clash if you hesitate.

Earth tones carry it: tobacco, camel, olive, rust, sand. Over them goes one colour pushed further than it should go, like plum trousers, a burgundy suit or red espadrilles under a floral dress.

The chips are sampled from the photographs themselves.

xi.Il laboratorio

Behind the ease, the pattern: paper cut to one body.

The technical home of the look is Neapolitan tailoring. Shoulders have no padding. The sleeve is gathered into the shoulder (spalla camicia), armholes are high, and the canvas is light. The jacket is built to look as though it was never built.

Rubinacci has made clothes in Naples since 1932. Kiton was founded in 1968. Isaia works from Casalnuovo, just outside the city.

xii.

Silk, folded without ceremony.

La materia

A good square is printed on silk, and its edges are rolled and stitched by hand. That small hem is the only proof of the work.

Then it gets pushed into the pocket and never folded. Drake’s in London is the house most people point to for squares and ties worn this way.

xiii.La maglia

Knit loose, worn looser.

An open crochet stripe, a skirt that doesn’t try to match. The hand shows in every row.

SolomeoUmbria

Solomeo is a medieval hamlet on a hill near Perugia. Brunello Cucinelli founded his cashmere company in 1978. In the 1980s he moved it here and restored the village around it: the theatre, the workshops, the gardens.

Here the ease extends past clothes to an entire place.

Brunello Cucinelli

Not only clothes: the house, the books, the long table.

xvi.L’idea

Practised ease, taken for a gift. The rehearsals happen; they just never show.

Its opposite isn’t carelessness. It is visible care.

Castiglione saw the worst fault as affectation, meaning effort put on display. Sprezzatura is how you avoid it. You still do the work. You just don’t ask anyone to admire it.

That is why the idea reaches beyond clothes. A pianist can seem to improvise, a host can serve a dinner that looks unplanned, and a sentence can read as if it wrote itself.

Regole

  1. Learn the rule properly, then loosen it.
  2. One undone detail, never three.
  3. Wear it until it stops being new.
  4. Nothing pre-tied, pre-folded, or sold as a set.
  5. Never be seen trying.
